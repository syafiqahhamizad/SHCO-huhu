# Staff Guide videos: Part A (everyone)

One short video per guide topic, 1–3 minutes each. Record the real portal, say what you click, and keep it to one topic per video.

## How to record (Mac)

1. Close other tabs and turn on Do Not Disturb, so no WhatsApp or e-mail pop-ups appear.
2. Open portal.shcolaw.com in Chrome at full size, signed in. Use your own account, or a staff account if you want to show what a lawyer sees.
3. Press **Cmd + Shift + 5** → **Record Selected Portion** → drag the box over the Chrome window → **Options → Microphone: MacBook microphone** → **Record**.
4. Follow the script below. Speak slowly and move the mouse slowly. Pause 1 second after each click.
5. To stop, click ⏹ in the top menu bar. The video is saved to the Desktop.
6. Upload it to **SHCO Portal › Staff Guide videos** in Google Drive. Use **Share → General access → Syafiqah Hamizad & Co** (anyone at the firm with the link) → Copy link.
7. In the portal, open **Staff Guide**, click **+ Video** on that topic and paste the link. A **▶ Watch video** button then appears for everyone.

Tips: record each video in one take, since a small mistake is fine. If a page shows client names you don't want in a video, use preview mode (`portal.shcolaw.com/?preview=1`), which shows sample data only.
